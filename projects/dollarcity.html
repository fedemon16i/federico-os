Context

Research at point of sale — turned into a ranked list, not a deck.

DollarCity is a value retail chain across Latin America. The brief was to understand the checkout and in-store experience at the point of sale — where friction actually lives, not where the brief assumed it did.

The output had one rule: a ranked list of actions the product team could act on immediately. Not a research presentation scheduled for two weeks later. Not a hundred-slide deck that gets opened once. A prioritized backlog from the field.

The problem with most research

Research that doesn't move the backlog isn't research — it's theatre.

  • Product team acts on evidence

Top findings

What the field actually showed

01

Checkout queue friction

Queue formation at peak hours wasn't a staffing problem — it was a layout problem. The scan station and bagging zone were positioned in a flow that forced shoppers to step backward, creating a physical bottleneck that compounded under load.

P1
02

Price uncertainty at shelf

Customers frequently picked up items without being sure of the price, then abandoned at checkout when the total didn't match expectation. The "$1.25 or less" value promise wasn't legible enough at shelf level.

P1
03

Staff product knowledge gaps

Staff redirection requests peaked in seasonal and new-arrival sections. Staff were aware of core stock but not of in-week arrivals — a training cadence problem, not a hiring problem.

P2

What I did

From observation to backlog

01

Field observation at POS

Hours of direct observation at checkout, not interviews about checkout. Watch what people do, not what they say they do. Friction shows up in behavior, not in self-report.

02

Contextual interviews

Short interviews right after the experience — while it's still in the body. Not scheduled focus groups with recruited participants a week later.

03

Synthesis → ranked list

Every observation mapped to a finding. Every finding assigned confidence (how many observations support it) and suggested action. Sorted by impact × feasibility, not by how interesting they were to write up.

04

Backlog-ready handoff

Output format matched the team's sprint planning format. No translation step. The research slotted directly into the backlog.
