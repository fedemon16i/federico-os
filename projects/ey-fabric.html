EY Fabric — Behavioral Analytics

Enterprise marketplace Behavioral analytics Pendo · PostHog · Session replay

Context

A catalog of 300+ items. No visibility into where people dropped.

EY Fabric is an internal marketplace where consultants pull tools, accelerators, and services into their workspaces. After a platform migration, product could no longer see how users moved through the catalog — or where they left.

Publishers were submitting catalog items through complex forms that asked for serial codes, equipment IDs, and billing references with no context. The cliff in submissions was visible in aggregate. Nobody knew which field caused it.

~50% Publisher item quality improved after instrumentation findings
E2E Full catalog journey instrumented end-to-end post migration
2 Fields identified as top exit points from 300+ form events

Before · After

The field that nobody finished

Forms shipped with no guardrails, no validation, and no field-level guidance. Pendo funnel + replay pinpointed the exact drop.

Before — form as shipped
  • People-picker tied to internal directories with no search hint
  • Service ID from a separate IT system, no explanation
  • Billing code requiring pre-approval — step not shown
  • Generic "Important Instruction" block (same text on every form)
  • No inline help anywhere. Error state appeared after submit.
  • No funnel instrumentation — drop-off invisible to product
After — Pendo findings applied
  • Inline contextual help on Contact Person and Service fields
  • Pre-configure checklist before form opens (billing pre-clearance)
  • Generic instruction block replaced with field-specific tooltips
  • Publisher item prompts: structured fields replaced free-form blanks
  • Full funnel instrumented — every field is a named event
  • Item quality audited and improved across publisher submissions

What I did

Instrument → find the cliff → spec the fix

01

Event taxonomy

Mapped every screen in the catalog and publisher journeys to named Pendo events before writing a single guide. Taxonomy first, dashboards second — so the data is queryable later, not just pretty now.

02

Funnel + session replay triage

Built short funnels on the publisher submission flow. Watched the sessions that failed. Grouped them by the shared struggle. Contact Person and the unmarked required service field emerged as the two top exit points — not billing, which was the assumed culprit.

03

Ranked backlog, not a deck

Findings went straight to the product backlog as a prioritised list: what field, how often, what the fix is. Not a research presentation to be scheduled and forgotten.

04

E2E instrumentation after migration

After platform migration broke the previous analytics setup, rebuilt event coverage end-to-end. The catalog journey — search, filter, preview, request — fully visible in PostHog and Pendo simultaneously.
